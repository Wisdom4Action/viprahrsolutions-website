VIPRA HR SOLUTIONS — WEBSITE PREVIEW
====================================

Included files:
- index.html: responsive one-page website preview and the three approved form structures
- privacy-policy.html: privacy page draft assembled from the approved policy document's known wording
- styles.css: shared design stylesheet
- assets/vipra-logo.png: supplied VIPRA HR Solutions logo

IMPORTANT STATUS
- This is a local preview package, not a published website.
- All form submit buttons are intentionally disabled.
- No candidate or employer information is transmitted or stored.
- Web3Forms is not configured.
- Before publishing, compare privacy-policy.html line-by-line against the exact approved policy document, insert the effective date, verify the operational inbox and current Web3Forms terms, and test every form.
- The website does not claim that VIPRA is an authorised TPA or a partner of Yashaswi/Distil. Eligibility notes identify provider-published guidance and caution that programme-specific requirements must be confirmed.
- No DNS or hosting settings have been changed.

LOCAL PREVIEW
